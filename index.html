Zoo Ticket Booking App

Figma — Full UX Case Study

A full redesign of the zoo ticket booking experience focused on reducing user drop-off and improving the flow for first-time visitors and families.

  • Simplified booking flow from 6 steps down to 3
  • Designed wireframes and interactive prototypes in Figma
  • Improved usability for first-time users and families through structured navigation
  • Conducted user research to identify and validate key pain points
